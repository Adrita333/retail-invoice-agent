# CLAIM SUBSTANTIATION GUIDELINES (internal - Blood Commercial Finance)

## G1. Purpose
G1.1 These guidelines tell the Key Account team what evidence is required before a claim is
settled, and what to do when it is missing.
G1.2 They do not override any retailer trade agreement. Where they conflict, the agreement
prevails.

## G2. Evidence by Claim Type
G2.1 **Promotional support** - promotion reference, agreed support value, and evidence the
promotion ran in the claimed period. Without a promotion reference the claim fails at clause 5.2
of the applicable agreement and no further review is required.
G2.2 **Co-op marketing** - a co-op reference and a copy of the executed activity or media plan.
G2.3 **Listing fee** - the SKU, the banner, and confirmation the SKU was not already charged a
listing fee in the current twelve-month period.
G2.4 **Damages** - annotated delivery note or timestamped photographs, and the damaged unit
count. The claimed value is checked against the damaged unit count at the invoiced unit price,
then against the damages allowance in clause 9.4 of the applicable agreement.
G2.5 **Shortage** - signed proof of delivery showing quantity received against quantity shipped.
G2.6 **Freight** - the documented additional cost incurred and evidence the Supplier missed the
agreed delivery window.
G2.7 **Price difference** - the invoice, the price applied, and the price the Retailer contends
should have applied under the current price list.

## G3. Missing Evidence
G3.1 A claim of US$150 or more submitted without the evidence required by G2 is held, not
rejected. The Retailer is asked once for the evidence.
G3.2 A claim below US$150 without evidence is settled under Appendix clause A2.3.
G3.3 A held claim with no evidence supplied within thirty (30) days is rejected.

## G4. Recomputation
G4.1 Every claim with a quantifiable basis is recomputed independently of the amount claimed.
G4.2 The recomputed entitlement is compared to the claimed amount and the variance assessed
against the tolerance in Appendix clause A2.1.
G4.3 Where the claim exceeds the recomputed entitlement beyond tolerance, the excess is the
leakage exposure and is recorded as such.

## G5. Citation Discipline
G5.1 Every rejection must cite the governing document, the clause number and the retailer's
contract reference.
G5.2 Where no clause supports a rejection, the correct outcome is "not established" and the
claim is escalated to a human, not rejected.
G5.3 A paraphrase is not a citation. The clause must be quoted.

## G6. What Must Never Be Automated
G6.1 Settlement above the Appendix clause A3.1 authority threshold.
G6.2 Rejection of a claim from a retailer currently in contract renegotiation.
G6.3 Any rejection that would be the third consecutive rejection for the same retailer in a
calendar month, given the commercial relationship risk.
