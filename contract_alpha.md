# TRADE AGREEMENT - CONTRACT_ALPHA
### Between Blood Femcare Pte Ltd ("Supplier") and the Retailers listed above ("Retailer")

## 1. Scope
1.1 This Agreement governs the supply of Supplier's femcare and infant-care products to the
Retailer in the Singapore market, and all commercial deductions, claims and trade allowances
arising from that supply.
1.2 Where a matter is not addressed here, the Trade Terms Appendix applies.

## 5. Promotional Support
5.1 Promotional support is payable only where the promotion was agreed in writing in advance
and carries a Supplier promotion reference number.
5.2 A claim for promotional support that does not quote a valid, pre-approved promotion
reference is not payable, irrespective of whether the promotion took place in store.
5.3 Promotional support is capped at the agreed support value recorded against the promotion
reference. Amounts in excess of the agreed value are not payable.
5.4 Where a promotion is extended by mutual agreement, the support value is not increased
unless a revised promotion reference is issued.

## 6. Listing and Range Fees
6.1 Listing fees are payable once per SKU per twelve-month period.
6.2 A repeat listing fee for the same SKU within the same twelve-month period is not payable.
6.3 Range review and shelf reset charges must be supported by a signed planogram.

## 7. Claim Submission
7.1 All claims must be submitted in writing, quoting the Supplier invoice number to which the
claim relates.
7.2 **Claims must be submitted within ninety (90) days of the date of the Supplier invoice to
which they relate. Claims submitted after this period lapse and are not payable.** No extension
of this period is granted by the Supplier's continued trading with the Retailer.
7.3 Each claim must be accompanied by supporting documentation appropriate to the claim type,
as set out in clause 8 and in the Supplier's Claim Substantiation Guidelines.
7.4 A claim that has been settled may not be resubmitted. Resubmission of a settled claim,
whether or not intentional, is recoverable by the Supplier under clause 12.
7.5 Deduction at source in advance of claim agreement is not permitted under this Agreement.

## 8. Supporting Documentation
8.1 Promotional support claims require the promotion reference and the agreed support value.
8.2 Damages claims require a delivery note annotated at the point of receipt, or photographic
evidence taken within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery.
8.3 Shortage claims require a signed proof of delivery showing the quantity actually received.
8.4 A claim submitted without the documentation required by this clause may be held pending
receipt of that documentation and does not accrue any entitlement in the interim.

## 9. Damages, Shortages and Returns
9.1 Risk in the goods passes to the Retailer on delivery.
9.2 Damage discovered at the point of delivery must be annotated on the delivery note before
the delivery is accepted.
9.3 Damage discovered after acceptance must be notified within forty-eight (48) hours.
9.4 **The Retailer's damages allowance is one point five per cent (1.5%) of the gross invoice
value of the consignment. Damages claimed in excess of 1.5% of gross invoice value are not
payable without prior written agreement of the Supplier.**
9.5 Goods withdrawn for quality or regulatory reasons are outside the clause 9.4 allowance and
are credited in full.

## 10. Freight and Logistics
10.1 Delivery is DDP to the Retailer's nominated distribution centre.
10.2 Freight recovery charges are payable only where the Supplier failed to meet the agreed
delivery window and the Retailer incurred a documented additional cost.

## 11. Pricing
11.1 The applicable price is the Supplier's published list price for the Singapore market, less
any trade discount recorded in Schedule 2.
11.2 Price difference claims must reference the invoice and the price the Retailer contends
should have applied.
11.3 Price changes require thirty (30) days' notice.

## 12. Recovery and Set-Off
12.1 The Supplier may recover any amount paid in respect of a claim later found not payable.
12.2 Recovery is effected by credit note reversal or set-off against future invoices.

## 13. Audit
13.1 The Supplier may audit the Retailer's claim records on reasonable notice.
13.2 **The Supplier may audit and reopen any settled claim within one hundred and twenty (120)
days of settlement.** This audit right is separate from, and does not extend, the claim
submission period in clause 7.2.

## 14. Payment Terms
14.1 Payment terms are sixty (60) days from invoice date.
14.2 An early settlement discount of three per cent (3%) applies where payment is received
within fourteen (14) days of invoice date.
