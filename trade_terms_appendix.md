# TRADE TERMS APPENDIX (applies to all retailer agreements)

## A1. Definitions
A1.1 "Claim" means any deduction, charge-back, credit request or invoice adjustment raised by
a Retailer against the Supplier.
A1.2 "Gross invoice value" means the invoiced value of the consignment before any deduction,
allowance or credit.
A1.3 "Settled" means a claim for which a credit note has been issued or a set-off applied.

## A2. Materiality and Tolerance
A2.1 A variance between the claimed amount and the Supplier's recomputed entitlement of five
per cent (5%) or fifty United States dollars (US$50), whichever is greater, is treated as
within tolerance and is not challenged.
A2.2 Tolerance under A2.1 is applied per claim. It may not be aggregated across claims to
justify a larger variance.
A2.3 Claims below US$150 in value are settled without individual substantiation review, on the
basis that the cost of review exceeds the exposure. This does not waive the Supplier's rights
under clause 12 of the applicable agreement.

## A3. Authority Matrix
A3.1 Claims up to US$2,000 may be settled by the Key Account Manager.
A3.2 Claims above US$2,000 require Commercial Manager approval before settlement, regardless of
whether the claim is otherwise valid and fully substantiated.
A3.3 Claims above US$10,000 require Finance Director approval.
A3.4 No automated system may settle a claim above the clause A3.1 threshold without a recorded
human approval.

## A4. Order of Precedence
A4.1 Where a retailer-specific trade agreement and this Appendix conflict, the retailer-specific
agreement prevails.
A4.2 Where a claim is governed by a retailer-specific agreement, the terms of any other
retailer's agreement are of no effect and must not be applied by analogy.
A4.3 Clause numbering is common across retailer agreements. Common numbering does not imply
common terms. The governing agreement must be identified from the Retailer's contract reference
before any clause is applied.

## A5. Duplicate Claims
A5.1 Two claims are treated as duplicates where they share the same Retailer, the same Supplier
invoice reference, the same claim type and the same claimed amount.
A5.2 The earlier-dated claim is treated as the original. The later claim is rejected.
A5.3 Where the original has already been settled, the later claim is recoverable under clause 12.

## A6. Dispute and Recovery Process
A6.1 A rejected claim is notified to the Retailer with the governing clause cited.
A6.2 A rejection that does not cite a governing clause may not be pursued in recovery.
A6.3 The Retailer has thirty (30) days to dispute a rejection.
A6.4 Historic acceptance rates for contested claims are tracked as the Recovery Acceptance Rate
and reported quarterly.
